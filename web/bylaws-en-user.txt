<h3>BYLAWS OF THE YAPBOZ ASSOCIATION</h3> Name and Headquarters of the Association

[MADDE 1]
Name of the Association: It is the “YAPBOZ Derneği” (YAPBOZ Association). Its short name is 'PuzzleDer'. The headquarters of the Association is in Istanbul. It has no branches.

[PARAGRAF]
The Objective of the Association and the Subjects, Forms, and Fields of Activity to be Pursued by the Association to Achieve this Objective

[MADDE 2]
The Association has been established to achieve the following objectives.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ я.]
To engage in activities and events to develop the Hobby sector, primarily related to jigsaw puzzles, in our country and to increase awareness of the concept.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ ii.]
To develop projects for the advancement of hobby culture in institutions and organizations operating in our country.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ iii.]
To ensure the development of industrial, commercial, and socio-economic relations among its members, who are representatives of companies producing, importing, marketing, and providing education and consultancy services for jigsaw puzzles.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ iv.]
To represent our country at the non-governmental organization level by participating in international studies and formations related to jigsaw puzzles.

[PARAGRAF]
In accordance with the objectives of the Association, to publish all kinds of materials, hold meetings, demonstrations, panel discussions, and organize competitions and events.

[BAŞLIK]
Subjects and Forms of Activity to be Pursued by the Association

[MADDE 3]
</b> All kinds of activities aimed at achieving the objective of the Association fall within the activity field of the Yapboz Association. Within this framework, to achieve its objective, the Yapboz Association;

[LİSTE_MADDESİ i.]
Conducts studies to increase domestic and foreign investments in the hobby field in Turkey.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ ii.]
Conducts studies to ensure that domestic or imported jigsaw puzzle products sold in our country comply with the relevant legislation and to prevent unsafe and non-compliant products from reaching the consumer.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ iii.]
Conducts studies in the fields of education, environmental protection, and social areas.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ iv.]
Conducts studies to increase foreign trade and diversify products and markets in order to create an open and competitive structure in the sector.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ в.]
Communicates and strategically cooperates with institutions and organizations working in line with common principles at the national and international level, and joins federations and other parent organizations when necessary. Within this framework, it can be a stakeholder, party, or participant in national and international projects.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ VI.]
May cooperate and form platforms with domestic and foreign private and official institutions, universities, chambers of commerce and industry, commodity exchanges, professional organizations, foundations, associations, unions, and similar organizations.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ VII.]
Directly participates in or effectively conveys its opinions to national and international policy-making and decision-making processes representing companies operating in the Hobby sector, including jigsaw puzzles.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ viii.]
Publishes a magazine at regular intervals to effectively convey the association's activities and achieve its founding objectives.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ ix.]
Uses all kinds of written and visual communication tools to directly convey and communicate its activities, views, suggestions, documents, reports, and similar elements to the public or relevant public institutions and organizations to achieve its objectives. Organizes meetings, seminars, congresses, conferences, and competitions.

[PARAGRAF]
The Yapboz Association (PuzzleDer) may acquire, establish, cancel, and release all kinds of personal, real, usufruct, lease, and pre-emption rights on real estate, including mortgages, by the decision of the Board of Directors in connection with activities suitable for its objective. It may record income to the Association, provided that the income it obtains is not distributed among its members and is used only in line with its own objective; it may establish economic enterprises. To achieve the objective of the Association, it establishes foundations when necessary; receives and gives aid and donations; can accept conditional and unconditional wills.

Field of Activity of the Association

[PARAGRAF]
The Association operates in the field of the jigsaw puzzle hobby.

Right to Become a Member and Membership Procedures

[MADDE 4]
</b> Every natural and legal person who has the capacity to act, adopts the aims and principles of the association, agrees to work in this direction, and who produces, imports, markets, provides education and consultancy services for, or does jigsaw puzzles has the right to become a member of the association. However, if a legal entity is to become a member, it must be the authorized manager or a company shareholder representing it. Foreign real persons must also have the right to settle in Turkey to become members. This condition is not required for honorary membership.

[PARAGRAF]
The membership application, to be made in writing to the presidency of the association, is resolved by the board of directors of the association within a maximum of thirty days as an acceptance of membership or rejection of the request, and the result is notified to the applicant in writing. The member whose application is accepted is recorded in the book kept for this purpose. The principal members of the association are the founders of the association and the persons accepted into membership by the board of directors upon their application. Those who have provided significant material and moral support to the association may be accepted as honorary members by the decision of the Board of Directors.

[BAŞLIK]
Resignation from Membership

[MADDE 5]
</b> Every member has the right to leave the association, provided that they notify it in writing.

[PARAGRAF]
The exit procedures are deemed to be concluded the moment the member's resignation letter reaches the board of directors.

[PARAGRAF]
Leaving the membership does not terminate the member's accumulated debts to the association.

[BAŞLIK]

Constantly avoiding assigned duties,

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 3.]
Failing to pay the membership dues within six months despite written warnings,

Formation, Meeting Time, and Call and Meeting Procedure of the General Assembly of the Association Article 9- The general assembly is the most authorized decision-making organ of the association; it consists of the registered members of the association.

[BAŞLIK]
The General Assembly;

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 1.]
Convenes ordinarily at the time specified in these bylaws,

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 2.]
Convenes extraordinarily within thirty days when deemed necessary by the board of directors or the supervisory board, or upon the written request of one-fifth of the association members. The ordinary general assembly convenes every 2 years, in May, on the day, place, and time to be determined by the board of directors. The general assembly is called to the meeting by the board of directors. If the board of directors does not call the general assembly to a meeting; upon the application of one of the members, the magistrate judge assigns three members to call the general assembly to a meeting.

[BAŞLIK]
Call Procedure

[PARAGRAF]
The board of directors prepares the list of members who have the right to attend the general assembly according to the association's bylaws. Members who have the right to attend the general assembly are called to the meeting at least fifteen days in advance, by announcing the day, time, place, and agenda in a newspaper or by notifying them in writing or via electronic mail. In this call, if the meeting cannot be held due to the lack of a quorum, the day, time, and place of the second meeting are also specified. The period between the first meeting and the second meeting cannot be less than seven days and more than sixty days. If the meeting is postponed for a reason other than the lack of a quorum, this situation is announced to the members in accordance with the call procedure made for the first meeting, stating the reasons for the postponement. The second meeting must be held within six months at the latest from the date of postponement.

[PARAGRAF]
Members are called to the second meeting again according to the principles stated in the first paragraph. The general assembly meeting cannot be postponed more than once.

[BAŞLIK]
Meeting Procedure

[PARAGRAF]
The general assembly convenes with the absolute majority of the members entitled to attend, and with the participation of two-thirds in cases of bylaw amendments and the dissolution of the association; if the meeting is postponed due to a lack of quorum, a majority is not sought in the second meeting. However, the number of members attending this meeting cannot be less than twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the supervisory board.

[PARAGRAF]
The list of members entitled to attend the general assembly is kept ready at the meeting place.

[PARAGRAF]
The identity documents issued by official authorities of the members entering the meeting place are checked by the members of the board of directors or the officials assigned by the board of directors. Members enter the meeting place by signing opposite their names on the list prepared by the board of directors. If the meeting quorum is met, the situation is recorded in a minute, and the meeting is opened by the chairman of the board of directors or one of the board members they will assign.

[PARAGRAF]
If the meeting quorum cannot be met, a minute is drawn up by the board of directors. After the opening, a council committee is formed by electing a chairman and a sufficient number of vice-chairmen and a secretary to manage the meeting. In the voting to be held for the election of the association's organs, it is mandatory for the voting members to show their identities to the council committee and sign opposite their names on the hazirun (attendees) list. The management and security of the meeting belong to the chairman of the council.

[PARAGRAF]
Only the items on the agenda are discussed at the general assembly. However, it is mandatory to include in the agenda the issues requested in writing to be discussed by one-tenth of the members present at the meeting. Every member has one voting right at the general assembly; the member must cast their vote personally. Honorary members may attend general assembly meetings but cannot vote. The issues discussed and the decisions taken at the meeting are written in a minute and signed jointly by the chairman of the council and the secretaries. At the end of the meeting, the minute and other documents are delivered to the chairman of the board of directors. The chairman of the board of directors is responsible for the protection of these documents and their delivery to the newly elected board of directors within seven days.

[BAŞLIK]
Voting and Decision Making Procedures and Methods of the General Assembly

[MADDE 10]
</b> In the general assembly, unless otherwise decided, the elections of the members of the board of directors and the supervisory board are voted secretly, and the decisions on other matters are voted openly. Secret votes are the votes collected by casting the papers or ballot papers sealed by the chairman of the meeting into an empty receptacle by the members after doing what is necessary, and determined by an open tally after the voting ends. In open voting, the method specified by the chairman of the general assembly is applied.

[PARAGRAF]
General assembly decisions are taken by the absolute majority of the members attending the meeting. Provided that, decisions on amending the bylaws and dissolving the association can only be taken by a two-thirds majority of the members attending the meeting.

[BAŞLIK]
Decisions Taken Without a Meeting or Call

[PARAGRAF]
Decisions taken with the written participation of all members without coming together and decisions taken by all the association members coming together without complying with the call procedure written in these bylaws are valid. Taking a decision in this way does not replace an ordinary meeting.

Formation, Duties, and Powers of the Board of Directors

[MADDE 12]
</b> The Board of Directors is elected by the general assembly as five principal and five substitute members. Decisions are taken by the absolute majority of the total number of members attending the meeting. In case of a vacancy in the principal membership of the board of directors due to resignation or other reasons, it is mandatory to call the substitute members to duty in the order of the majority of votes received in the general assembly.

High Advisory Council, Duties, and Powers

[PARAGRAF]
Article 15– The High Advisory Council is the highest advisory organ of the Association.

[PARAGRAF]
The High Advisory Council consists of a total of 5 members, including Principal, Honorary, and Honorary members elected by the General Assembly. The previous term board of directors chairman and past term chairmen are natural members of the council. If the number of past term chairmen is more than 5, the members are elected by the general assembly from among the past term chairmen.

[PARAGRAF]
The other members of the High Advisory Council are elected by the general assembly of the association from among the members who have been members of the association for at least five (5) years. The Presidential Board of the High Advisory Council, consisting of three members, is elected for a period of two years at the General Assembly meeting. The Presidential Board consists of a chairman, a vice-chairman, and a secretary member. With the invitation of the President of the Council, it convenes on the dates it deems appropriate, negotiates its topics, and concludes them. In addition, if the board of directors requests, it can also convene extraordinarily with the invitation of the Chairman of the Board of Directors.

[PARAGRAF]
The chairman of the High Advisory Council is also the ordinary publication board member of the magazine, which is the publication organ of the association. The High Advisory Council elects at least one more member other than the chairman as the publication board member of the magazine. The High Advisory Council may appoint more members to the publication board of the magazine if the Board of Directors approves.

[PARAGRAF]
The Ombudsman of the Yapboz Association (PuzzleDer) is elected for a two-year period by the High Advisory Council from among the members of the High Advisory Council.

[PARAGRAF]
The Council's decisions are of an advisory nature and are not binding on the Board of Directors. Representatives of the Legislative, Executive, and Judicial organs, non-governmental organizations, professional organizations with public legal personality and their parent organizations, members of academic institutions, and association members who are not members of the Council may be invited by the Presidency of the Council to exchange views at the High Advisory Council meetings according to the nature of the agenda.

[PARAGRAF]
Council correspondences are carried out by the association secretariat. If the Council President and members wish, they may attend board of directors meetings without the right to vote.

Bookkeeping Principles and Procedures of the Association and Books to be Kept

[MADDE 17]
Bookkeeping principles;

[PARAGRAF]
In the association, books are kept according to the operating account basis. However, if the annual gross income exceeds the limit specified in Article 31 of the Associations Regulation, books shall be kept on a balance sheet basis starting from the following accounting period. In case of switching to the balance sheet basis, if it falls below the above-mentioned limit in two consecutive accounting periods, it is possible to revert to the operating account basis starting from the following year. Regardless of the above-mentioned limit, books may be kept on a balance sheet basis by the decision of the board of directors. If a commercial enterprise of the association is opened, a separate book is kept for this commercial enterprise in accordance with the provisions of the Tax Procedure Law.

[BAŞLIK]
Recording Procedure

[PARAGRAF]
The books and records of the association are kept in accordance with the procedures and principles specified in the Associations Regulation.

[BAŞLIK]
Books to be Kept

[PARAGRAF]
The following books are kept in the association.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ я.]
The books to be kept on the operating account basis and the principles to be followed are as follows:

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 1.]
Decision Book: The decisions of the board of directors are written in this book in order of date and number, and the bottom of the decisions is signed by the members attending the meeting.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 2.]
Member Registry Book: The identity information of those entering the association as members, and the dates of entry and exit from the association are processed into this book. The amount of entrance and annual dues paid by the members can be processed into this book.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 3.]
Document Registration Book: Incoming and outgoing documents are recorded in this book with their date and sequence number. The originals of the incoming documents and the copies of the outgoing documents are filed. Documents incoming or outgoing via electronic mail are preserved by taking a printout.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 4.]
Fixture Book: The date and form of acquisition of the fixtures belonging to the association, the places where they are used or given, and the deregistration of those whose usage periods have expired are recorded in this book.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 5.]
Operating Account Book: Incomes received and expenses incurred on behalf of the association are recorded clearly and regularly in this book.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 6.]
Receipt Document Registry Book: The serial and sequence numbers of the receipt documents, the names, surnames, and signatures of those who receive and return these documents, and the dates they received and returned them are recorded in this book.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ ii.]
The books to be kept on a balance sheet basis and the principles to be followed are as follows:

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 1.]
The books recorded in sub-clauses 1, 2, 3, and 6 of clause (a) are also kept in case of bookkeeping on a balance sheet basis.

[LİSTE_MADDESİ 2.]
Journal Book, General Ledger, and Inventory Book: The procedure for keeping these books and the method of recording are carried out according to the principles of the Tax Procedure Law and the General Communiqués on Accounting System Application published based on the authority granted to the Ministry of Finance by this Law.

[BAŞLIK]
Certification of Books

[PARAGRAF]
In the association, the books that are mandatory to be kept must be certified by the provincial associations directorate or a notary before starting to use them. The use of these books continues until their pages run out, and intermediate certification of the books is not performed. However, it is mandatory to have the books kept on a balance sheet basis and the books with forms or continuous form sheets certified again every year in the last month preceding the year in which they will be used.

[BAŞLIK]
Preparation of Income Statement and Balance Sheet

[PARAGRAF]
In case records are kept on an operating account basis, an “Operating Account Table” (specified in Annex-16 of the Associations Regulation) is prepared at year-ends (December 31). In the case of bookkeeping on a balance sheet basis, a balance sheet and income statement are prepared at year-ends (December 31) based on the General Communiqués on Accounting System Application published by the Ministry of Finance.

Income and Expense Transactions of the Association

[MADDE 18]
Income and expense documents;

[PARAGRAF]
Association revenues are collected with a "Receipt Document" (an example of which is found in Annex-17 of the Associations Regulation). In case the association revenues are collected through banks, documents such as receipts or account summaries issued by the bank serve as a receipt document. Association expenses are made with spending documents such as invoices, retail sales receipts, and self-employment receipts. However, for the payments of the association that are within the scope of Article 94 of the Income Tax Law, an expense note is issued according to the provisions of the Tax Procedure Law, and for its payments that are not within this scope, an "Expense Receipt" (an example of which is found in Annex-13 of the Associations Regulation) is issued. Free deliveries of goods and services to be made by the association to individuals, institutions, or organizations are made with an "In-Kind Aid Delivery Document" (an example of which is found in Annex-14 of the Associations Regulation). Free deliveries of goods and services to be made by individuals, institutions, or organizations to the association are accepted with an "In-Kind Donation Receipt Document" (an example of which is found in Annex-15 of the Associations Regulation).

[BAŞLIK]
Receipt Documents

[PARAGRAF]
"Receipt Documents" to be used in the collection of association revenues (in the format and size shown in Annex-17 of the Associations Regulation) are printed at the printing house by the decision of the board of directors. Regarding the printing and control of receipt documents, taking delivery from the printing house, recording them in the book, the handover between former and new treasurers, and the use of these receipt documents by the person or persons who will collect revenues on behalf of the association with a receipt document and the delivery of the collected revenues, action is taken according to the relevant provisions of the Associations Regulation.

[BAŞLIK]
Authorization Certificate

[PARAGRAF]
The person or persons who will collect income on behalf of the association are determined by a board of directors decision, also specifying the authorization period. An "Authorization Certificate" (an example of which is in Annex-19 of the Associations Regulation) containing the clear identity, signature, and photographs of the persons who will collect income is issued in three copies by the association and approved by the chairman of the association's board of directors. One copy of the authorization certificates is given to the associations units. Changes regarding the authorization certificate are notified to the associations unit within fifteen days by the chairman of the board of directors. Persons who will collect income on behalf of the association can only start collecting income after a copy of the authorization certificates issued in their name is submitted to the associations unit. Regarding the use, renewal, return, and other matters of the authorization certificate, action is taken according to the relevant provisions of the Associations Regulation.

[BAŞLIK]
Retention Period of Documents

[PARAGRAF]
Excluding books, the receipt documents, spending documents, and other documents used by the association are preserved for 5 years in accordance with the number and date order in the books they are recorded in, reserving the periods specified in special laws.

[BAŞLIK]

[BAŞLIK]
Notification of Immovables

[PARAGRAF]
Immovables acquired by the association are notified to the civil administration authority by filling out the "Immovable Property Notification" (presented in Annex-26 of the Associations Regulation) within thirty days from their registration at the land registry.

[BAŞLIK]
Notification of Receiving Aid from Abroad

[PARAGRAF]
In case aid is to be received from abroad by the association, they fill out the "Notification of Receiving Aid from Abroad" (specified in Annex-4 of the Associations Regulation) in two copies and notify the civil administration authority before the aid is received. A copy of the board of directors' decision taken regarding receiving aid from abroad, if any, the protocol, contract, and similar documents drawn up on this subject, and a copy of the receipt, extra, and similar documents regarding the account to which the aid was transferred are also attached to the notification form. Cash aids must be received through banks and the notification requirement must be fulfilled before use.

[PARAGRAF]
Notification Regarding Joint Projects Carried out Together with Public Institutions and Organizations A copy of the protocol and the project related to the joint projects carried out with public institutions and organizations on issues related to the association's field of duty is attached to the "Project Notification" (shown in Annex-23 of the Associations Regulation) and submitted to the governor's office of the place where the association's headquarters is located within one month following the protocol date.

[BAŞLIK]
Notification of Changes

[PARAGRAF]
The change occurring in the settlement place of the association is notified to the civil administration authority within thirty days following the change by filling out the "Settlement Place Change Notification" (specified in Annex-24 of the Associations Regulation); changes occurring in the association organs outside the general assembly meeting are notified by filling out the "Notification of Changes in Association Organs" (specified in Annex-25 of the Associations Regulation). Amendments made in the association's bylaws are also notified to the civil administration authority attached to the general assembly results notification within thirty days following the general assembly meeting where the bylaw amendment was made.

[BAŞLIK]
Internal Audit of the Association

[MADDE 21]
In the association, an internal audit can be conducted by the general assembly, the board of directors, or the supervisory board, as well as an audit can be assigned to independent auditing firms. The fact that an audit has been conducted by the general assembly, the board of directors, or independent auditing firms does not eliminate the obligation of the supervisory board. The audit of the association is carried out by the supervisory board at least once a year. The general assembly or the board of directors may conduct an audit or assign independent auditing firms to conduct an audit when deemed necessary.

[BAŞLIK]
Borrowing Procedures of the Association

[MADDE 22]
If needed to realize the association's objective and carry out its activities, the association can borrow by the decision of the board of directors. This borrowing can be on the subject of purchasing goods and services on credit, or it can be done in cash. However, this borrowing cannot be made in amounts that cannot be covered by the income sources of the association and in a nature that will put the association into payment difficulties. The General Assembly is authorized for borrowings exceeding this, provided that they do not exceed half of the latest definitive account. The right of borrowing authority that the General Assembly may give to the board of directors against a project is reserved.

[BAŞLIK]
How the Bylaws Will Be Amended

[MADDE 23]
Bylaw amendments can be made by a general assembly decision.

[PARAGRAF]
A 2/3 majority of the members entitled to attend the general assembly is required to make a bylaw amendment in the general assembly. If the meeting is postponed due to a lack of quorum, a majority is not sought in the second meeting. However, the number of members attending this meeting cannot be less than twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the supervisory board.

[PARAGRAF]
The decision majority required for a bylaw amendment is 2/3 of the votes of the members attending the meeting and having the right to vote. The vote for bylaw amendment is held openly in the general assembly.

[BAŞLIK]
Dissolution of the Association and the Form of Liquidation of Its Assets

[MADDE 24]
The general assembly can always decide to dissolve the association.

[PARAGRAF]
In order to discuss the issue of dissolution in the general assembly, a 2/3 majority of the members entitled to attend the general assembly is required. If the meeting is postponed due to a lack of quorum, a majority is not sought in the second meeting. However, the number of members attending this meeting cannot be less than twice the total number of members of the board of directors and the supervisory board.

[PARAGRAF]
The decision majority required for a dissolution decision to be taken is 2/3 of the votes of the members attending the meeting and having the right to vote. The dissolution decision vote is held openly in the general assembly.

[BAŞLIK]
Liquidation Procedures

[PARAGRAF]
When a dissolution decision is given by the general assembly, the liquidation of the money, property, and rights of the association is made by the liquidation board consisting of the last board of directors members. These procedures start from the date the general assembly decision regarding the dissolution is taken or the case of spontaneous termination becomes finalized.

[PARAGRAF]
During the liquidation period, the phrase "Yapboz Association in Liquidation" is used in the name of the association in all transactions. The liquidation board is assigned and authorized to complete the liquidation procedures of the association's money, property, and rights from beginning to end in accordance with the legislation. This board first examines the association's accounts. During the examination, the books, receipt documents, spending documents, land registry, and bank records, and other documents belonging to the association are determined, and its assets and liabilities are recorded in a minute. During the liquidation processes, a call is made to the creditors of the association, and if any, its goods are converted into money and paid to the creditors. If the association is a creditor, the receivables are collected. After the collection of receivables and payment of debts, all remaining money, property, and rights are transferred to the place determined in the general assembly. If the place to be transferred is not determined in the general assembly, it is transferred to the association in the province where the association is located, which is closest to its objective and has the highest number of members on the date it was dissolved.

[PARAGRAF]
All transactions regarding liquidation are shown in the liquidation minute, and liquidation procedures are completed within three months, except for the additional periods granted by the civil administration authorities based on a justifiable reason. Following the completion of the liquidation and transfer procedures of the association's money, property, and rights, it is mandatory for the liquidation board to report the situation in a letter to the civil administration authority of the place where the association's headquarters is located within seven days, and to attach the liquidation minute to this letter. The last board of directors members, in their capacity as the liquidation board, are tasked with preserving the books and documents of the association. This duty can also be given to one board of directors member. The retention period for these books and documents is five years.

Lack of Provision

[MADDE 25]
In matters not specified in these bylaws, the provisions of the Law on Associations, the Turkish Civil Code, and the Associations Regulation issued in reference to these Laws, and other relevant legislation regarding associations apply.
